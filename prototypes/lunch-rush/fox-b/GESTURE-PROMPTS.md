# Gesture generation prompts

Built-in ChatGPT image tool; no CLI or external video generator.

Common specification: preserve the approved waiting-v2 fox identity. Generate one landscape sheet with exactly four columns and three rows of equal square cells, on white with no grid lines or text. Each cell is the next pose in reading order. Keep camera, scale, torso root and waist baseline fixed. Only connected arms, paws, eyes, mouth and ears articulate. Both shoulders and paws remain visible. Include anticipation, gesture, reaction and settling. Use the celebration sheet as layout reference for the other four sheets.

## waiting

A friendly wave: both paws visible, right paw raises from waist to beside face, wrist waves left then right twice, lowers again. Left paw stays gently on chest. One blink midway. End in starting pose.

## speaking

An explaining gesture: both paws start near chest, right forearm extends with open palm toward viewer, left paw opens too, both hands gesture alternately then return. Mouth alternates closed smile, small open speaking mouth, rounded mouth, then smile. Slight eyebrows lift. End in starting pose.

## thinking

A thoughtful chin scratch: right paw moves from chest to chin, gently rubs twice, eyes glance upward, eyebrows become thoughtful, then paw lowers back to chest. Left paw stays visible across tummy. End in starting pose.

## surprised

A friendly surprised reaction: both paws start relaxed near chest, ears perk up and eyes widen, mouth forms small O, both forearms lift with open palms facing viewer, face relaxes to delighted smile, paws settle to chest. End in starting pose.

## celebrating

A lively two-paw cheer: paws at chest with smile, both arms extend upward in triumph, elbows bend and fists pump down and back up twice, eyes squeeze joyfully and mouth opens in laughter, then arms relax and smile returns. No jumping. End in starting pose.

The wave playback reorders the generated cells to keep the raised paw on the same side throughout.
