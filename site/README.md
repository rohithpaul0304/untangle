# Untangle website (Aardvark-style long page) · v1

Files: `index.html` (the page, fonts embedded), `lesson.html` (chapter one, same build as brilliant-grammar/index.html; the page loads it with `?web=1`), `site.html` (source with `__FONTS__` and asset placeholders).

Structure, top to bottom: cinematic entry (full-screen, press-and-hold to advance six scenes: 'You already play this game', the robbery, the inspector, the offer, the two rooms, the name; +20 XP per scene shown top right; Skip intro; ends on 'Play as Ravi', which slides the whole thing up and scrolls to the phone; the XP earned is passed into the lesson; shown once per session, and never under prefers-reduced-motion); sticky pill nav; lime hero with the headline, two CTAs and the playable lesson inside a phone frame (the real lesson, not a video); marquee of topics; "How it works" block (three tilted step cards); "Chapters" (1 free and playable, 2 to 4 locked to the app); "We've got a game for every kind of mess" big-word block; plum "Why Untangle" with the rotating ring; FAQ accordion; mint app block with store badges (links are placeholders); footer with the trademark note. A "Continue in the app" pill floats in once the hero is scrolled past, and the lesson posts `{untangle:'done', xp}` to the page when chapter one is finished.

Lesson web mode (`lesson.html?web=1`): chapter one only, ends on the chapter list with 2 to 4 locked, "Continue in the app" targets `#app` on the parent page. Without the flag, the same file has all three chapters for the app build.

To do before launch: real store links (`data-store="ios"` and `"android"` in the app block), analytics, a real favicon and OG image, verify the Jio and tobacco dates, confirm logo usage with each brand.

The app section is now a real 3D board game box rendered with Three.js (threejs/box.js, bundled with esbuild into box.bundle.js and inlined into index.html by build.py). Option A: butter lid with the cover art drawn on a canvas texture (wordmark, tagline, player/time/age pills, cobalt subject pill, the fox), navy tray walls with cream interior, navy underside printed with "Think inside the game." and the App Store badge, folded chapter board, fox token on station one, three subject cards and a cobalt die. Directional key light with soft shadow map onto a shadow-only ground, hemisphere fill, ACES tone mapping. Scroll drives the lid hinge (0 to 112 degrees, eased), a slow camera orbit and a slight box turn; the section is pinned for 320vh. The section background is the paper cream so the butter lid separates. Reduced motion shows the open state. An HTML App Store pill fades in under the box when open so the call to action stays tappable. Rebuild: edit threejs/box.js, run `npx esbuild box.js --bundle --minify --format=iife --outfile=box.bundle.js`, then `python3 build.py`.

v16 (9 October 2026): the 3D box was not rendering since v11. Cause: a variable (`dirty`) used by the fox placement before its declaration inside `mount()` in threejs/box.js, which threw at start-up and left the canvas empty. Fixed by declaring the render state first. Added a fallback for browsers or embeds without WebGL (and for any future mount error): `frames.js` captures the box at 12 lid positions with a transparent background (`?capture=1` turns on preserveDrawingBuffer), stored in assets.json as `box_frames` (about 560 KB); the page swaps frames by scroll position when WebGL is unavailable. Page weight is now about 2 MB, most of it the frames and Three.js; acceptable for one page, worth revisiting with external files once hosted.
