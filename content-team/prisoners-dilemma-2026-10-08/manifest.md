## Approved direction

Robbery with accomplice Ravi → caught and separated → disclosed fictional sentence deal → choice and exploration of both perspectives → compare one-person and joint changes → name and explain prisoner's dilemma → apply in a simplified café-discount situation. Use accomplice, not ambiguous partner. First choice is unscored. Goal assumption: each minimizes their own prison years. Never invent Ravi's decision in response to learner choice. Confess/confess is an illustrative branch, not guaranteed human behavior. No hidden-rule twist. Café numbers are authored, not market evidence. Prison years (you/Ravi): silence/silence 1/1; confess/silence 0/5; silence/confess 5/0; confess/confess 3/3. Café daily profits (you/rival): neither discounts 5000/5000, only you 6000/2000, only rival 2000/6000, both 3000/3000.
