# One statement. Two rooms. — learner inputs

Status: Draft

A fictional game. You and Ravi robbed a jewellery store. Now you’re caught, in separate rooms. Get the fewest prison years for yourself. You can change your statement, but you cannot talk to Ravi. Neither sees the other’s final statement.

The police offer you both the same deal. Confess and name Ravi, or stay silent and refuse the deal. Zero means no prison years.

| You | Ravi | Your years | Ravi’s years |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Silent | Silent | 1 | 1 |
| Confess | Silent | 0 | 5 |
| Silent | Confess | 5 | 0 |
| Confess | Confess | 3 | 3 |

1. Imagine Ravi stays silent. Choose: **Stay silent / Confess**.
2. Imagine both confess: 3 years each. Ravi stays confessing; only you become silent. Predict your years: **Fewer than 3 / Still 3 / More than 3**.
3. Back to both confessing. Whose statement changes? **Only I stay silent / Only Ravi stays silent / We both stay silent**.

New role: you run one café; another owner runs a nearby café. Seek greatest own daily profit, meaning money left after costs. Choices are independent, with neither seeing the other’s final choice. One fictional day; no future effects or unlisted costs.

| You | Other café | Your profit | Other profit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Usual | Usual | ₹5,000 | ₹5,000 |
| Discount | Usual | ₹6,000 | ₹2,000 |
| Usual | Discount | ₹2,000 | ₹6,000 |
| Discount | Discount | ₹3,000 | ₹3,000 |

4. The other café runs a discount. Choose: **Run a discount / Keep usual prices**.
5. Compare from both discounting. Change: **Only I keep usual prices / We both keep usual prices**.

Now each café running a discount pays an extra ₹2,000 campaign cost. Usual prices incur none. Everything else stays unchanged. Final profits:

| You | Other café | Your profit | Other profit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Usual | Usual | ₹5,000 | ₹5,000 |
| Discount | Usual | ₹4,000 | ₹2,000 |
| Usual | Discount | ₹2,000 | ₹4,000 |
| Discount | Discount | ₹1,000 | ₹1,000 |

6. The other café runs a discount. Choose: **Run a discount / Keep usual prices**.
7. Why did the better choice change? **The campaign cost changed / The other price changed**.
8. Cost still applies; the other café keeps usual prices. Choose: **Run a discount / Keep usual prices**.
