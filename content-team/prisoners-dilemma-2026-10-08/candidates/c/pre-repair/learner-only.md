# Teach the fox a fix — learner-only
Status: Draft

This packet contains setup, visible rules and choices only.

## s01
You and your accomplice Ravi robbed a jewellery store. You’re caught, in separate rooms. Get the fewest prison years for yourself. You control only your statement.

Fictional game
You cannot talk to Ravi. Neither of you sees the other’s final statement before choosing. You have no way to make Ravi keep a promise.

Your two statements
Stay silent: refuse the deal. Confess: accept it and name Ravi.

## s02
Here’s the whole deal. Ravi also wants the fewest years for himself. What statement do you make?

Both stay silent
You: 1 year · Ravi: 1 year

Only you confess
You: 0 years · Ravi: 5 years

Only Ravi confesses
You: 5 years · Ravi: 0 years

Both confess
You: 3 years · Ravi: 3 years
Your goal: the fewest prison years for yourself. Zero = no prison years.

- Stay silent
- Confess
