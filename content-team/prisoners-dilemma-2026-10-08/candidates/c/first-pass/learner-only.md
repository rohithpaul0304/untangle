# Teach the fox a fix — learner-only
Status: Draft

This packet contains setup, visible rules and choices only.

## s01
You and Ravi robbed a jewellery store. You’re caught, in separate rooms. Your goal: fewer prison years. You control only your statement.

One fictional decision
Neither sees the other’s final statement. No messages or enforceable agreement.

Your two statements
Stay silent: refuse the deal. Confess: accept it and name Ravi.
