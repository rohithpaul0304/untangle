# One switch. Two rooms.

Status: Draft

You and Ravi robbed a jewellery store. You’re caught, in separate rooms. Your goal here: fewer prison years. You control your statement. Neither sees the other’s final statement. No contact or enforceable agreement. One fictional decision; switches are labelled what-ifs.

Confess means accepting the invented deal and naming Ravi. Stay silent means refusing the deal. Zero means no prison years. This assigned goal is not a judgment of anyone’s values.

| Your statement | Ravi’s statement | Your years | Ravi’s years |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Silent | Silent | 1 | 1 |
| Confess | Silent | 0 | 5 |
| Silent | Confess | 5 | 0 |
| Confess | Confess | 3 | 3 |

First what-if: Ravi stays silent. Choose: **Stay silent** / **Confess**. Your selection is unscored. Then change only your own statement.

Separate what-if: both confess. If only you become silent while Ravi still confesses, will your years **fall**, **stay**, or **rise**? Unscored.

From both confessing, controls available for comparison: **Silence only Ravi** / **Silence both**. These are teaching comparisons, not new control over Ravi in the story.

## Café setting

You run a café near another owner’s café. Goal: greatest own daily profit—money left after listed costs. Fictional one-day model. Independent choices, neither sees the other’s final choice, no future effects or other costs.

| Your action | Other café | Your profit | Other profit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Usual prices | Usual prices | ₹5,000 | ₹5,000 |
| Discount | Usual prices | ₹6,000 | ₹2,000 |
| Usual prices | Discount | ₹2,000 | ₹6,000 |
| Discount | Discount | ₹3,000 | ₹3,000 |

Start with both discounting. Other café stays fixed discounting. Choose: **Keep usual prices** / **Run a discount**. Then hold your chosen action while comparing the other café at usual prices.

## Separate cost what-if

Each café running a discount now pays an additional ₹2,000 that day. Usual prices incur none. Everything else stays unchanged. These are final profits after that cost.

| Your action | Other café | Your profit | Other profit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Usual prices | Usual prices | ₹5,000 | ₹5,000 |
| Discount | Usual prices | ₹4,000 | ₹2,000 |
| Usual prices | Discount | ₹2,000 | ₹4,000 |
| Discount | Discount | ₹1,000 | ₹1,000 |

Other café fixed discounting. Choose: **Keep usual prices** / **Run a discount**. Explain the effect of the added cost while holding the other café fixed.
