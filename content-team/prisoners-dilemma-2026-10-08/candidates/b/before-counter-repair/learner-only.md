# One switch. Two rooms.

Status: Draft

You and Ravi robbed a jewellery store. You’re caught, in separate rooms. You want the fewest prison years. You choose only your statement. You cannot talk to Ravi or make a deal with him. Neither of you can see the other’s statement before choosing. Ravi wants the fewest years for himself, too.

Fictional game. Confess means accepting the deal and naming Ravi. Stay silent means refusing the deal. Zero means no prison years.

| Your statement | Ravi’s statement | Your years | Ravi’s years |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Silent | Silent | 1 | 1 |
| Confess | Silent | 0 | 5 |
| Silent | Confess | 5 | 0 |
| Confess | Confess | 3 | 3 |

Imagine Ravi stays silent. Choose: **Stay silent** / **Confess**. Then change only your own statement.

Imagine you both confess. If only you become silent while Ravi still confesses, will your years **fall**, **stay**, or **rise**?

Imagine starting from both confessing: **Silence only Ravi** / **Silence both**.

## Café setting

You run a café near another owner’s café. You want the most profit today—money left after costs. You choose separately, without seeing the other’s choice. Only today counts; all costs are included.

| Your action | Other café | Your profit | Other profit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Usual prices | Usual prices | ₹5,000 | ₹5,000 |
| Discount | Usual prices | ₹6,000 | ₹2,000 |
| Usual prices | Discount | ₹2,000 | ₹6,000 |
| Discount | Discount | ₹3,000 | ₹3,000 |

Imagine you both discount. The other café keeps discounting. Choose: **Keep usual prices** / **Run a discount**. Then hold your chosen action while comparing the other café at usual prices.

## Imagine one new cost

Each café running a discount now pays an additional ₹2,000 that day. Usual prices incur none. Everything else stays unchanged. These are final profits after that cost.

| Your action | Other café | Your profit | Other profit |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Usual prices | Usual prices | ₹5,000 | ₹5,000 |
| Discount | Usual prices | ₹4,000 | ₹2,000 |
| Usual prices | Discount | ₹2,000 | ₹4,000 |
| Discount | Discount | ₹1,000 | ₹1,000 |

The other café keeps discounting. Choose: **Keep usual prices** / **Run a discount**. Explain the effect of the added cost while holding the other café fixed.
