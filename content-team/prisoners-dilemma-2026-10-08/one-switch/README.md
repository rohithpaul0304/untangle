# One switch. Two rooms. — playable build

Status: Draft · AI-checked (both prison variants and full ten-day runs driven in headless Chromium, zero errors) · not yet founder-played · not human-tested.

`index.html` is the whole game in one file: art, faces, fox, engine, save. Open it on a phone or host it anywhere (any static host; no server, no build step). About 350 KB.

## The sequence

1. Title → Play.
2. The rooms (6 taps in the tight version): the job → caught → the inspector's deal on one screen with the offer card visible → you choose under uncertainty → "Ravi talked." (he always does in round one; both branches are fair) → his chair → your chair and the name: prisoner's dilemma.
3. Tomorrow exists: one intro screen, then ten days on one screen each. Six prices (₹50–₹100), 200 customers, ₹50 cup cost, Meera starts at ₹100 and copies your previous day's price. Move the price and the customers walk between the cafés before you commit; one tap opens the day. The ten-day strip, both totals, Meera's face and the fox's reaction are always on screen.
4. Ten days: strip, totals, the fox's reaction to day ten (including "Last day. No tomorrow. You grabbed the exit, same as the rooms." when earned), the recap of what you actually did, and the benchmark. Run again to compare two runs side by side.
5. What did I learn: three beats and the takeaway. End.

## Switches

- `?prison=approved` plays the prison act word for word as approved on 8 October 2026 (14 screens, decision at tap 7). Default is the tight version (6 screens, decision at tap 3).
- `?author=1` shows a scene menu and a copy-log button for playtests.
- Progress saves on the device; "Start over" needs two taps.

## What it reuses

Scene art and the expression sheet generated by the content team; the fox stills; the 200-customer café rule from the team's adaptive café; the approved prison copy as the alternate variant.
