# Founder direction — 8 October 2026 (after playing candidate B, generated-art revision)

Status: Founder-approved direction (quoted); the flow that follows it is Draft until the founder plays it.

Founder, verbatim: "Don't want anything that says 'imagine Ravi is silent' or anything. This has to be bleeding. Don't rewire things. It has to flow. At the end of it, we can probably create a loop of 3, 4, or 10 times where this whole thing is repeated, and we can understand how the behaviour changes. Probably, there can be expressions on Ravi's and the other person's face also."

## What this changes

1. **No hypothetical framing.** No "Imagine Ravi stays silent", no "Imagine both discounting", no "Reset · before", no rewinding a state to show a what-if. Things happen in the world and the learner lives with them. Comparisons come from three honest sources only: the deal itself (a character states it once, and it stays visible as a prop), what another character actually did, and what the learner actually does across rounds.
2. **Bleeding.** The consequence must land on the learner: five years because you kept a deal he broke; three each because you both grabbed the same exit. Characters react to what happened. No neutral narration of cells.
3. **Flow.** One continuous story with a signalled time jump, not a slideshow of rule screens. The earlier same-day correction "explain each rule in a separate beat" is refined: context first still holds, but the deal is one in-world event (the inspector's offer), not four screens.
4. **The loop is the ending.** After the one-shot dilemma is named, the same shape repeats for 3, 4 or 10 rounds with a visible history, so the learner sees behaviour change: theirs and the other person's. This supersedes the brief's "no repeated games" for the ending only; the one-shot dilemma must still land first.
5. **Expressions.** Ravi, the inspector and the café owner have expression states tied to outcomes, produced the same way as the fox's stills (identity reference, consistent sheet). Never idle; a face changes only when something happened.

## What does not change

The story (jewellery store, accomplice Ravi, separate rooms), the prison and café numbers, the generated art direction, the fox, the status words, the locked chapter-one opening. Numbers stay checkable; Ravi's round-one move is authored and the same for both learner choices, never a reaction to the learner.
