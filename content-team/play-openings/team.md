# Independent Play Design Team

Requested 7 October 2026. This is a multi-agent workflow launched on request, not a persistent background process.

Three designers receive the same goal, project history and constraints. Each chooses one concept and interaction independently. They cannot see or discuss each other's drafts before submission. Concepts may differ; comparison evaluates candidates as whole experiences, not a controlled experiment isolating mechanics.

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Play Designer A | One opening, every choice branch and second move |
| Play Designer B | Same brief; separate independently chosen opening |
| Play Designer C | Same brief; separate independently chosen opening |
| Independent reviewer | Infer role, goal, controls and outcomes from learner-only scripts; identify actual defects |
| Coordinator | Preserve drafts, check model arithmetic/branches, arrange revision, compare candidates and report limits |

Shared brief: adults with short attention spans; useful PM concepts; no report-editing worksheet. Clear role and stakes before action. Visible rules before scored choice. Exact conversational responses and visual cause/effect. A discovery should improve the next move. Preserve the approved game-theory opening. No full chapter or production app change.

Each opening targets about a minute, not a measured duration. Agent review is a simulation of interpretation. It cannot establish human delight, voluntary continuation, retention or how animation feels. Human testing remains pending.
