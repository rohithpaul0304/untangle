# Fix the photo booth

🦊 “You run a festival photo app. Six friends want their photos. You have two upgrades. Get as many photos to their phones as you can.”

Six photo tiles wait beside three connected machines:

**Receive: 4 → Clean up: 2 → Send: 4**

*Limits are photos per run.*

“One run goes left to right, once. Each machine handles up to its limit; leftovers stay beside that machine. One upgrade adds 1 to a machine’s limit. You can put both upgrades together.”

Two upgrade pegs: **+1** **+1**

Move each peg onto a machine, or tap a peg then a machine. You can move them again before running.

**Photos delivered: 0 of 6**

**Run the booth** — available when both pegs are placed.

---

## After you run: show only the matching consequence

The machines run left to right. Photos move through the machines and onto friends’ phones. Waiting photos stay visible beside their next machine.

**If both pegs are on Receive:** Two photos reach phones. Four wait before Clean up. 🦊 “You received all six. Four are still waiting for cleanup.”

**If both pegs are on Clean up:** Four photos reach phones. Two wait before Receive. 🦊 “Four reached their phones. Now all three machines have the same limit.”

**If both pegs are on Send:** Two photos reach phones. Two wait before Receive; two before Clean up. 🦊 “Sending got faster. But only two photos reached it.”

**If pegs are on Receive and Clean up:** Three photos reach phones. One waits before Receive; two before Clean up. 🦊 “Three arrived. Two are stuck at cleanup; one hasn’t entered.”

**If pegs are on Receive and Send:** Two photos reach phones. One waits before Receive; three before Clean up. 🦊 “Both ends got faster. Three photos still wait in the middle.”

**If pegs are on Clean up and Send:** Three photos reach phones. Two wait before Receive; one before Clean up. 🦊 “Three arrived. The extra sending space stayed empty.”

## Group photos

🦊 “Now six group photos arrive. Group-photo mode has different limits. Your two upgrades are free again. Can you deliver four?”

*Previous run finished.*

Six new photos wait. Machines are empty.

**Receive: 4 → Clean up: 3 → Send: 3**

**Bottleneck**

Same rules: run left to right once; each upgrade adds 1 to a limit. Leftovers stay beside their next machine.

Two upgrade pegs: **+1** **+1**

Place both. **Run the booth.**

---

## After you run: show only the matching consequence

**Both on Receive:** Three delivered; three wait before Clean up. 🦊 “Receiving all six didn’t clear the cleanup queue.”

**Both on Clean up:** Three delivered; two wait before Receive, one before Send. 🦊 “Cleanup is faster. One photo now waits at Send.”

**Both on Send:** Three delivered; two wait before Receive, one before Clean up. 🦊 “Send has room. Cleanup can only pass it three.”

**Receive and Clean up:** Three delivered; one waits before each machine. 🦊 “Send still stops the fourth photo.”

**Receive and Send:** Three delivered; one waits before Receive, two before Clean up. 🦊 “Cleanup still stops the fourth photo.”

**Clean up and Send:** Four delivered; two wait before Receive. 🦊 “Four delivered. You raised both tied limits together.”

If three delivered: **Move upgrades** resets this group-photo run. **Finish** shows one peg moving onto Clean up and one onto Send, then the four-photo run.

If four delivered: **Finish**.
