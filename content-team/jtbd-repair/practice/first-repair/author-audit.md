## Lexical-match self-audit
- FILM-A/B are demonstrations with consequences, not proof of inference. Their choices deliberately make the changed deadline concrete.
- GA requires explaining two opposing choices with price held constant. “Collection” is tempting from the list but lacks ownership evidence. EXPLORE carries real evidence from the first situation plus an unsupported price account; this distractor may still be easier than ideal.
- GB cannot be solved by matching “ten minutes” to a job. Arrival resolves feasibility; it does not establish preference. Its correct response is longer than the others by a few words, so answer-length sensitivity still needs checking.
- QA is the one observational insufficient-evidence item. Parents, bags and a clean kitchen support neither offered motive over the other. No stereotype fills the gap. GB separately limits a deterministic prediction; it does not introduce a second sparse observation.
- QB pits two evidenced purposes against each other. The saved restaurant supports one; the revealed work followed by rejection supports the other. The learner must weigh a sacrifice, not select a repeated sentence. Outcome is a leading investigation, not certainty.
- QC asks for a product move with a visible customer action. The old group offer is feasible in time but mismatched to a now-single repeat request. “Last Tuesday” repeats between evidence and offer because the action must be identifiable; this is still the easiest main item, not a strong standalone transfer measure.
- R1/R2 remain simple recognition checks by preservation instruction. They cannot establish that a learner can weigh competing evidence. This limits the correction strength for the repaired questions.

## Remaining gates and weaknesses
This is a text repair, not an app, approved opening or passed fun gate. No unfamiliar learner has played it. Do not claim enjoyment, learning or mastery. An independent checker must receive only practice-learner.md and, when needed, corrections-learner-v0.2.md; keys stay separate.
Two film predictions plus five checks may run long; measure rather than promise three to five minutes. The paired fictional outcomes simplify real buying decisions. The Netflix/Blockbuster frame shows service fit in two circumstances, not a causal account of company success or failure. The opening author must avoid duplicating these exact questions and answers before practice.
R1 corrects purpose/activity confusion less directly than the evidence-weighing errors in QB; the preserved finite fallback and current worked path supply the missing explanation. GB challenges overconfidence but offers certainty at both wrong ends, which may cue a testwise answer. This should be tested before shipping. All consequences are authored, not observed customer behaviour.
