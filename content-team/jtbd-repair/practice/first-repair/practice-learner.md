## Two situations

### FILM-A · Planning ahead
Fox: “You're helping Arun choose a film service. It's Monday. His next film night is Saturday.”

Netflix's DVD-by-mail service and Blockbuster's rental shops were real. This is a simplified historical setting; Arun and his choices are invented. There is no streaming in this scene.

Arun has watched everything by a director he likes. He makes a list of three unfamiliar films by other directors. None is in the nearby shop. All three can arrive by post before Saturday. The shop has a familiar favourite he could collect today. The two offers cost the same in this exercise.

Fox: “Which offer would you put in front of him first?”

- POST: Send the three-film list by post.
- SHOP: Reserve the favourite at the shop.

Choose before viewing the next beat. This first prediction is unscored.

### FILM-B · Guests soon
Fox: “A week later, same Arun. This time two friends will arrive in an hour.”

Arun opens his three-film list, then closes it when he sees the postal arrival date: Tuesday. He checks the nearby shop. It has the familiar favourite; collection takes 20 minutes. His friends have already agreed to watch it. Both offers still cost the same. He has no other playable film at home.

Fox: “Which offer would you put in front of him now?”

- POST: Send the three-film list by post.
- SHOP: Reserve the favourite at the shop.

Choose before viewing the next beat. This prediction is also unscored.

## Guided Q&A

### JTBD-GA-03 · Read the change
Fox: “Arun didn't become a different person. Which account best explains both choices?”

- EXPLORE: He always wants an unfamiliar film; he accepts the shop as a cheaper substitute.
- OCCASION: He is seeking discovery on Monday and a usable shared evening when friends are due.
- COLLECTION: He is building a collection; he changes suppliers to get more titles.

### JTBD-GB-03 · Check your explanation
Fox: “Suppose Tuesday delivery becomes delivery in ten minutes, at the same price. What can we conclude?”

The postal offer now arrives before the friends. You have no new evidence about which film they would prefer; their agreement to the familiar favourite still stands.

- SWITCH: Arun will definitely switch to the three-film list.
- POSSIBLE: Both offers can meet the deadline; his next choice is still open.
- STAY: Arun will definitely keep the shop reservation.

## Quiz

### JTBD-QA-03 · The bags at six
Fox: “You're deciding what a meal app should show Leena. Here's all you have so far.”

At six, Leena receives takeaway bags. Her parents are visiting. The kitchen is clean. You have not heard why she ordered, or seen what she did beforehand.

Which explanation can you support?

- TALK: She ordered to spend the evening talking with her parents.
- TREAT: She ordered to give her parents a restaurant meal as a treat.
- OPEN: Those are two possibilities; this observation doesn't separate them.

### JTBD-QB-03 · One more thing to notice
Fox: “Now watch what happens before Leena pays.”

Leena compares two orders. Both feed four, arrive at seven and cost the same. One is a new restaurant she has saved for months. The other is an ordinary meal requiring no serving or washing up. She removes the saved restaurant's order when she learns its dishes need heating and plating. She buys the ordinary meal and puts away the serving dishes.

Which purpose does this choice give you more reason to investigate first?

- OCCASION: Make the visit special through a long-awaited restaurant meal.
- HOST: Get everyone fed without taking on the serving and clearing work.

### JTBD-QC-03 · Change what the app shows
Fox: “Another visit, another decision. You choose which offer gets the top spot.”

This time Leena says, “My parents have eaten. It's just me, and I'll have ten minutes between calls. Last Tuesday's order was ideal. I want that again.” Last Tuesday she ordered a single meal. Both options below can reach her in time. Each shows its price before she pays.

- GROUP: Show “Feeds four · ready in 30 minutes,” opening a choice of group meals.
- AGAIN: Show “Order last Tuesday's meal again,” opening that exact single meal for confirmation.

Which offer would you show first, based on this evidence?
