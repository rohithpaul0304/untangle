### JTBD-GA-04
Type: evidence ranking, conditional on supplied observation. Answer: DISCOVERY.
- ERRAND: “Post does save a trip. But Arun first made a list the shop couldn't supply. Avoiding an errand doesn't explain why those particular films made the list.” [INVENTED_MOTIVE → R2]
