Status: Draft.

## After the concept: afternoon

Imagined afternoon: a parent buys a shake after lunch. The child takes a few slow sips, then goes back to chatting. Half leaves on the tray.

- I put two samples on the counter.

You can offer less shake with the same slow sips, or the full amount with quicker sips. Which afternoon sample do you put on the menu?

Imagined sample rules: smaller cup, same recipe, fewer slow sips. Full cup, thinner recipe, more quick sips. Both can be finished alongside the meal.

What you saw: lunch already eaten; a few slow sips, then back to chatting.

- I launch the smaller, thick shake.
- I launch the full-size, thinner shake.
