# JTBD teaching correction

Status: Draft. Coordinator reread the founder's HBS article on 7 October 2026 after the founder asked where JTBD was in the proposals.

Source: https://online.hbs.edu/blog/post/jobs-to-be-done-examples

Core: explain why someone chooses a solution in particular circumstances. Identify the progress they seek, the alternatives they could use, and why the chosen solution fits. A product, a feature and an activity are not automatically the job.

Functional, emotional and social considerations can matter. Establish them from observation and customer evidence; do not invent a hidden emotional motive to create a twist. The article recommends reflection, observation and interviews.

Teaching correction: the recent pitches foregrounded feature choices and surprising reveals. Those can illustrate JTBD, but learners must first reason about the customer's circumstances and purpose, then connect that understanding to product decisions. Naming the framework after a puzzle does not by itself teach it.

Before another storyboard, specify what learners must identify and explain. Keep all current premise recommendations provisional; this correction does not select the homebuyer case or reopen rejected interview-prep decks.
