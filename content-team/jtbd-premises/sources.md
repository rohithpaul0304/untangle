- Milkshake account: purchases concentrated before 8:30 a.m.; those customers ordered no other menu items. Long-lasting, one-handed consumption served commute hunger and boredom. Suggested changes include prepaid checkout and thicker consistency. These are proposed improvements; no measured recipe-versus-sales outcome is established here.
- Historical Netflix/Blockbuster account: immediate movie-night collection versus DVD-by-mail discovery and viewing on personal schedules, with no late fees/deadlines. Use the documented contrasts; do not present them as mutually exclusive capabilities, universal customer types, or sufficient proof of either company's success/failure.

Player controls, visual comparisons and illustrative timelines are lesson adaptations, not historical customer experiments. No fabricated sales, performance figures or testimonials. Keep citations out of learner dialogue.
