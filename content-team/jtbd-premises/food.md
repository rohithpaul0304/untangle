# Food/commute premise: the slower milkshake
Status: Draft

**Recommendation: retain for comparison.** This is stronger than substitution: the same customer needs speed and slowness at different points.

**Source facts.** HBS reports most milkshake purchases before 8:30 a.m., with no other menu items. Commuters wanted hunger relief and something lasting through the drive. Its recommendations include prepaid checkout and thicker consistency. These are recommendations, not verified sales improvements. Source: https://online.hbs.edu/blog/post/jobs-to-be-done-examples (coordinator-verified).

**Exact opening line.** “You run a fast-food chain. Your team can make milkshakes easier to drink—or make them take longer. Which change do you launch?”

**First choice.** **“Make it thinner.” / “Make it thicker.”** This is an unscored first instinct. Both alter the product; neither gets a verdict before the customer situation appears.

**Visible consequence.** A clearly labelled illustrative commute follows the choice. No minutes, sales figures, or invented study results.

- **Thinner:** The cup empties while the journey continues. “Your shake finishes first.”
- **Thicker:** The cup drains more slowly and remains alongside more of the journey. “Your shake lasts further into the drive.”

Show the morning purchase pattern, then explain the reported hunger-and-boredom job. Neither animation establishes that a specific recipe lasts an entire commute.

**Earned discovery.** “Easy to drink” is not automatically “better.” The customer is buying help getting through a journey. Then change the decision: shorten checkout or leave it alone? Prepaid checkout removes waiting without shortening the useful part. Name Jobs to be Done after this distinction emerges.

**Why continue.** Can the learner distinguish time the customer wants removed from time the product must help them fill?

**Failure risk.** A familiar case may reveal the answer immediately. Without the checkout reversal, this collapses back into feature matching. Human interest remains untested.
