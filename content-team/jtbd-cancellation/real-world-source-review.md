1. In **Recommended ending**, replace **“organising a mailed catalogue”** with **“organising a film catalogue and DVD delivery.”** The source describes DVD-by-mail and a personalized catalogue. It does not say a printed catalogue was mailed. The current wording risks teaching the wrong delivery model.
2. In the same paragraph, replace **“discovering less obvious films over time”** with **“discovering films matched to personal tastes over time.”** The source contrasts personalized discovery with immediate movie-night rental; “less obvious” adds a claim about the films that the supplied account does not establish.
