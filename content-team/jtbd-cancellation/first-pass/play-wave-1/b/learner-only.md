## Her first help

Fox: Repair her next rehearsal. Which help should come first?

Structured rehearsal: Record the whole answer; stop at 2:00. Replay, organise opening/evidence/result, retry. No surprise questions.

Follow-up rehearsal: Answer varied unseen questions; replay responses. No complete timed opening or help organising it.

Both work privately when she is free. Neither has a price or time advantage. You can combine them later.

- Load structured rehearsal
- Load follow-up rehearsal
