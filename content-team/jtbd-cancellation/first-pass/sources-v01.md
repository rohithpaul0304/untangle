## Boundaries for the brief

- The two opening signals create a question, not its answer. A scored move needs enough customer evidence to reason from; the decisive fact must not be concealed until after scoring.
- Separate the product action, such as practising answers, from the progress the particular customer seeks. Do not make “get hired” the universal job of every interview-practice user.
- A fresh case should change the circumstances or desired progress so the learner must infer the job again. Repeating the cancellation pattern would check recall of a story rather than use of the concept.
- Omit historical business figures, rankings, executive titles and success claims. The article’s recent update does not independently verify them. Its Netflix/Blockbuster account may illustrate different customer situations, but cannot establish a single cause of either company’s outcome; it is unnecessary for this hook.
- Citations remain in internal source notes, not learner dialogue. No human-testing claim is earned by this review.
