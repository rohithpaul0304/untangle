## What is already sound

- One actionable capability: infer progress from circumstances, then justify suitable help. The distinction between product activity and customer progress is worth teaching; no taxonomy or retention lesson is needed.
- The opening permits uncertainty and cannot be graded. The customer statement resolves this customer's cancellation without claiming the app caused hiring.
- Evidence, intervention capabilities and constraints must precede scoring; feasible equivalent answers receive credit. These directly address **“Grading an uninformed guess on a hidden rule”** and **“Arbitrary restriction to force the answer.”**
- The second move preserves desired progress while changing the obstacle, so the best-supported help can change. The parcel case changes the relevant constraint to coordination and uses a feature request: it passes the premise test against **“Same example with new nouns called transfer.”**

## Handoff blockers and bounded pass conditions

**1. The brief does not separate diagnostic evidence from the solved example.** In lines 33–39, the evidence includes both the exact intervention Mira used and its successful reported effect, immediately before asking what help fits. The job statement and explanations are also not marked as author-only or post-response. A designer can faithfully produce answer recognition with no useful inference. This risks **“Payoff explained before the move,” “Obvious advice in plain words,”** and **“Report editing and draggable fractions.”**

**Pass condition:** Add a short disclosure boundary: pre-decision facts include her circumstances, current performance and the capabilities/constraints of possible help; the supplied job formulation, the recommendation and the successful-use report are answer-key or post-response material. The learner must contribute at least a link from observed facts to desired progress and the remaining obstacle before that interpretation is confirmed. Accept uncertainty at the hook. Keep its response an unscored hypothesis; interpreting an already-known cancellation is not itself a prediction of an observable outcome.

**2. “Show what help permits” leaves the causal demonstration unfixed.** Lines 41 and 47 require a visible consequence, but do not settle what changes, what remains unresolved, or which observations would justify the contrast. Three designers could invent different efficacy rules or merely reveal the answer in text. This risks **“Explaining a change that is not shown,” “Forced actions dressed as decisions,”** and hidden-rule grading.

**Pass condition:** Teacher supplies a compact, author-facing consequence specification for the initial and changed-obstacle cases: help selected → opportunity it actually provides → observable remaining gap. Fix any fictional response assumptions here before design. Make clear which facts justify scoring fit and which later observations only illustrate it. Neither successful learning nor hiring may be the guaranteed consequence. Require competing help to offer a credible benefit, avoiding **“Prudent-versus-careless options.”** This is a content boundary, not a prescribed visual, control or opening lens.

**3. The brief flags the time jump without fully specifying its meaning.** Cancellation is now; the help decision revisits the past; the second comparison changes that past. “Keep the time period explicit” at line 67 does not establish whether the learner is reconstructing history or changing it. Mira has already reported a successful intervention and no future interviews. The PILLARS failure **“Why did the story jump?”** remains possible.

**Pass condition:** Mark the preparation choice as a hypothetical reconstruction of the earlier need, not an intervention offered after cancellation. Mark the second condition as an alternate preparation comparison, not a new event contradicting “no other interviews planned.” Specify the explanatory connection: the earlier need accounts for what practice was serving; the comparison tests whether help should change when that need's obstacle changes. Preserve acknowledgment of the hook response and of each decision, per **“A choice with no acknowledgment.”** Designers still own the narrative treatment.

## Learning design after those repairs

The parcel transfer should require the learner to state the needed progress and justify a feasible solution before seeing the supplied interpretation. Do not teach that maps or customer requests are inherently wrong. Its feasibility rules already belong before grading.

Keep one customer and one decision goal through the main case; avoid making the job offer, cancellation, two-minute performance and follow-up ability simultaneous targets. The later practice plan should mark an unsupported motive, app activity substituted for progress, or an infeasible recommendation as review-needed. After a second difficulty, give an explained worked path and a fresh changed case, per **“Withholding teaching after a wrong attempt.”** No practice set is required in this phase.

**Release condition:** Re-review the revised disclosure boundary, two consequence specifications and explicit time relationships. Do not pass on a promise that designers will resolve these. Candidate play will subsequently judge whether the story earns attention; this review cannot certify that.
