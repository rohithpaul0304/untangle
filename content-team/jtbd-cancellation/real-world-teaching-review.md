## Decision

**Pass for an ending recommendation and optional example bank. No teaching blocker in this scope.** This does not select the founder's final example or authorize a script, deck or build.

- The Netflix/Blockbuster connection is substantive: the same category can serve different progress because timing and discovery needs differ. DVD-by-mail's inability to solve an immediate evening need gives that contrast a concrete limit. This extends the existing situation–progress–help relationship without requiring another framework.
- The early-2000s DVD-by-mail setting is explicit. The purpose replacement usefully separates fictional teaching cases from the source's historical account.
- The parcel task remains the independent transfer check. The company account is an illustrative closing application, not evidence of learner competence. No compulsory brand quiz or five-example exercise is specified.
- The practical exit asks for a fact-based comparison and a circumstance that could reverse it. That is more useful than remembering a company name, and it extends the already taught move rather than introducing a separate skill.
- The source account is not presented as proof of commercial success or a universal account of customers. Engagement remains a hypothesis. These boundaries address **“Claiming fun, engagement or retention from an AI review.”**

## Retained risks for candidate review

**Length:** The actual recommendation adds one brief application; it does not require the five-item bank. A candidate that makes all five compulsory, adds another scored sequence, or repeats the full teaching cycle here would exceed this reviewed ending. The bounded correction would be to retain one historical contrast and the practical exit, leaving the bank in author reference. Check **“A whole chapter of choose → explain → card”** and **“Every sentence its own tap.”** No extra compulsory response is needed to earn this ending.

**Depth:** The short bank entries are starting points, not five ready lessons. In particular, “food without leaving home” or “colleagues connect remotely” could become **“Obvious advice in plain words.”** Only Netflix/Blockbuster receives enough contrasting circumstances here to support the recommended ending. Selecting another bank example would require its own concrete connection, not brand substitution.

**Continuity and evidence:** A designer must make the company comparison feel like a consequence of the discovery, not an unrelated history interlude. The text supplies that connection; actual flow remains untested. Do not treat “Netflix won” as the explanation, or treat all users of either service as having one job. Any later scored adaptation needs its own visible-facts and answer review; this pass does not preapprove one.
