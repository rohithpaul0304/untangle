In the previous video, I introduced you to the definition of what a game is and what is considered as a game. In this video, I'd like to start looking at the basic ideas on how the game is solved. For that, let me introduce you to the concept of dominant strategy. What is a dominant strategy? If there is a strategy out there that I would like to play, irrespective of what my rivals would do, I would call this as dominant strategy. Let's recollect what a game is. Every player has access to a set of strategies.
That's what we discussed in the previous video, correct? Now, among those set of strategies that I have access to, if there is one strategy out there that I would fall back on, irrespective of what the other players in the game would do, that is what I call as dominant strategy. From a practical sense, what does it mean when I have dominant strategy? I will play dominant strategy. Let's go back to the two games that we discussed in the previous video to understand what the dominant strategy is about. Let's actually start with voluntary contribution game. Let's just quickly recap. There are five players.
Each player has 10 rupees, and each player contributes an integer amount of money out of these 10 rupees to a common kitty. Each player is entitled therefore to 16 rupees. Let's look at the case of A. A has not contributed single rupee, but yet he or she has gotten 16 rupees back. Therefore, A is left with 26 rupees. How much has B, D, and E got? Sixteen rupees each. So let's actually understand this.
When B, C, D, and E choose to contribute entire 10 rupees, A not contributing anything gives A 26 rupees. But had A contributed as well, A will end up with 20 rupees. So therefore if A, B, C, D, and E have contributed nothing, it is better for A to also contribute nothing as against contributing 10 rupees. Therefore, what do we call this contributing nothing to be? This is your dominant strategy. When everyone contributes 10 rupees, it's better for me to contribute nothing. When everyone contributes nothing, it is still better for me to contribute nothing. Irrespective of what the rest of the players do, it is always in my best interest to contribute nothing in this game. So that's why I call contributing nothing to be a dominant strategy.
