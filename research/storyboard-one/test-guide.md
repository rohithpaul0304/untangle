# Newcomer test — not yet conducted

Send only learner-deck.html to someone who has not seen the project or this conversation. Open it in a browser. Do not send storyboard.md, memory.md, or this guide beforehand. The deck is a sequence of still storyboard frames, not the finished app.

## Invitation

“Please try this short learning story without help from me. We are testing the explanation, not you. If anything is confusing, say so. You can stop whenever you want.”

## Session

1. Let them proceed at their own pace. Do not explain the rules beyond what is written in the deck.
2. At the advertising choice, ask “What are you trying to do?” Record their exact answer without correction.
3. Let them make their choice and view the consequences. The deck records their choice locally in memory for this session.
4. They answer why the same action produced two different outcomes BEFORE seeing the named concept and authored explanation.
5. They see the explanation, then answer the different workplace situation.
6. Ask where they first felt unsure, and whether they wanted to continue. Accept a negative answer without defending the story.
7. Ask them to use Download my responses and share the resulting text file. The deck sends nothing automatically and stores no name or contact details. Closing or reloading clears an unfinished session.

Do not screen-record people without their consent. No recruitment messages have been sent. No paid research service is required.

## Scoring — facilitator only

Explanation before teaching:
- **Understands:** identifies rival ads stopping, loss of its exclusive advertising advantage, and customers returning in this model. Exact vocabulary unnecessary.
- **Partial:** says “the rival changed” or repeats 2/5 without connecting customer movement.
- **Not yet:** says our own ad cost fell again, customers brought more coins each, or cannot explain.

Transfer after teaching:
- **Understands:** their own arrival stays fixed while others' lateness changes waiting time or meeting start. Outcome depends on others too.
- **Partial:** recognizes others are late without explaining the impact on the player.
- **Not yet:** concludes this proves arriving on time is bad or cannot connect the two situations.

Record need for prompting separately. Do not turn a coached answer into a pass. If they say the market feels unrealistic, retain that criticism separately from whether they understand the rule.

## Interpretation

One unfamiliar participant can expose a failure, not validate market appeal or retention. If they misunderstand the cause, revise that beat before adding animation. If they understand but feel bored, improve stakes and meaningful action rather than hide the rules. Aim to repeat with three to five unfamiliar people before expanding.
