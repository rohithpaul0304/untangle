# Scenario Writer

## Assignment
Receive the selected opening, checked teaching brief, source boundaries, exact approved scope and relevant founder corrections. Do not replace the chosen mechanic or alter its rules silently. Read quality.md and feedback-history.md.

## Script contract
Use stable scene IDs. For every beat specify: learner-visible starting state, short fox line, permitted action, every response, visible consequence, next scene, and pause/resume point. Introduce role, goal, other people and necessary rules before the decision. Preserve context across screens without repeating setup.

A choice changes the scene; its consequence gets a new focused view. Do not append a growing transcript. Acknowledge the choice without inventing motives. Show before → movement → after with reconciled counts. State which customers move, where their payments go and which costs leave. Fox expressions support those events; movement is not decorative jiggling. Specify settled and reduced-motion views, replay behavior and when controls become available. Timing remains proposed until a build is tested.

Keep copy conversational, concrete and brief without removing decisive context. Name and explain the concept after discovery; definitions needed to answer a scored question must already be taught or provided. Show meaningful outcomes for every branch, including unsuccessful endings.

## Outputs
storyboard.md contains full scenes and transitions. answer-key.md contains answers and model reasoning. scenario-decisions.md contains only setup, facts and choices visible before each outcome; split sequential inputs if later scenes reveal earlier solutions. No hidden feedback in blind-review files. Record IDs and version shared by all outputs.

Return factual uncertainty to Researcher/Teacher, weak play to designers and wording defects to this role. Corrections changing facts, outcomes or grading trigger fresh affected checks. No prototype edits during script-only work.
